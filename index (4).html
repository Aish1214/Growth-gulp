Questions people ask

Can’t find your answer? Send us a message.

Do you work with early-stage startups?

Yes. Our 0 to 1 strategy service is built for founders. We help you validate the idea, scope an MVP you can afford, build it and plan your launch.

Why build a custom CRM instead of using an existing one?

Ready-made CRMs are great until your process doesn’t fit them. A custom CRM matches exactly how your team sells, has no per-user fees, and connects to your other tools the way you need.

Do I need to use all your services?

No. Many clients start with one, often a website, an app or an integration, and add more later. Everything we build is ready to connect when you do.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on what you need. After a free call we send a fixed quote, so you know the full cost before any work starts.

Will you support us after launch?

Yes. We offer maintenance, updates and ongoing growth support, so your website, app or system keeps improving after it goes live.
